Frog & Bear Lore

Topic of page: The important background facts and interests of Frog (Treefrog) and Bear (Harleybear)

General interests

Frog

Treefrog (Frog/Frawg) is an enthusiast of strange-looking men and currently pondering why weird women aren't famously coupled. She enjoys fantasy movies from the 80s, big hair, and glitter. She is currently compiling the wardrobe of a dramatic soap opera widow sans husband. She formerly worked in a library known for its portal into strange shadow rooms. She worked on set design for the lost web series Doorways, prequel to the production where she met Harley.

She has been known to speak positivity about Jeff Goldblum and Greg Davies from Taskmaster.

Bear

Harley (Bear/Harleybear) is a noted clown-lover. She used to be a competitive jouster in the Pan-Canada Grand Prix in her younger days and worked on the currently-lost Backdoor Tapes (not porn - an experimental arthouse series where she and Treefrog met). Recently she has denied rumors that she has a crush on Michael "Avantris" Gilder, as she does not enjoy his famous Chuckles persona. Treefrog continues to recommend she watch Prime so she can experience his villain voices. She is a known knitter, a strange compulsion that is being tracked by multiple agencies as a sign of deviance.
